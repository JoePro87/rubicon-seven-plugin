# D100 Tables — delegate to the engine

The Monster Lair and Landmarks d100 tables are **certified engine data**. Don't roll them from
a table here — call the engine and weave the result.

- **Monster Lair** (d100 — who lairs here, where, and the omen that hints at it)
  → `roll(action="location", location_type="monster_lair")`
- **Landmark** (d100 — a striking feature dominating the horizon)
  → `roll(action="landmark")`

Light generative surface: a lair is a threat *and* a place with a history; a landmark is a
navigation point *and* a hook. Roll, then make it matter in the fiction.
