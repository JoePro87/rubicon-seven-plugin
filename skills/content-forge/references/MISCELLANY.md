# Miscellany Generators

Book-faithful transcription of the CH Referee's Toolbox miscellany generators
(printed pp.221-226): books, petty gods, trade caravans, fine clothing, fine
dining, and rival adventurers. All d20 unless noted; roll each column
independently. Engine-relevant loot (Gifts, elixirs, codices, poisons,
cybernetics, exotica) delegates to the ENGINE GENERATOR CONTRACT (SKILL.md).

## Engine handoff

Rival-adventurer Gifts, elixirs, codices, poisons, drugs, crucibles, and exotic
weapons all delegate to the engine generators (ENGINE GENERATOR CONTRACT,
SKILL.md). A generated rival party can be statted with the **Monster Generator**
(MONSTERS.md) or built from PC-ancestry data; register significant rivals as
NPCs (`generate(action="npc")` for names, then a sheet/lorebook entry).

*(Not in the preview PDF as standalone generators: assassins, autarchs, fine
art, musical instruments — referenced elsewhere in the book. Add when the full
edition lands.)*
